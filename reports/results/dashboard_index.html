How these numbers are made

Each experiment's final model plays every opponent in a fixed roster under protocol rating_v1. Pairwise results come from the evaluation summary of each run; ratings are Bayesian Bradley–Terry fits per roster, so ratings from different rosters are compared through the Lucario rule bot present in each. Health metrics come from each PPO cycle's health, training and canary records. All files are exported from the repository's reports/runs by ptcg_exp.export_results.
